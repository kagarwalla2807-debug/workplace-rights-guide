General information only

Workplace Rights Guide explains Australian workplace law in plain English. It is general information, not legal advice, and is not affiliated with any government agency. For your situation, contact the Fair Work Ombudsman (external site) on 13 13 94 or a legal service.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 000.
